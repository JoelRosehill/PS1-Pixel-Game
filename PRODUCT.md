# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Keyboard-and-mouse players exploring a browser-based dark fantasy action RPG.

## Product Purpose

Chromatic Odyssey combines fast spellblade combat with tranquil exploration and
page-based spell progression. The five pillars in docs/GDD.md govern development.

## Brand Personality

Mesmerizing, peaceful, forceful. Peaceful landscapes contrast with violent combat.
Elden Ring informs exploration; Ultrakill informs mobility, as specified in the GDD.

## Anti-references

Avoid global pixel filters, conventional mana pools and UI that obscures combat.
Preserve the established game world and pixel HUD rather than introducing website styling.

## Design Principles

- The physical book represents progression, not just an inventory icon.
- Exploration earns spells; aggression earns the Momentum used to cast them.
- Show controls, costs and feedback where players make decisions.
- Preserve movement flow and legible combat silhouettes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific conformance target is recorded in the project brief. Implementation
defaults: keyboard-operable menus, visible focus, text alongside colour cues,
legible contrast, reduced UI motion and paused reading/selection screens.
