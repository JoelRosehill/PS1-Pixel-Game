# Job 8 — Colossal Bosses

## Delivered

- Boss framework (`src/enemies/bosses/Boss.ts`): phases with roaring transitions, weighted
  move scheduling, per-move parry windows, posture breaks, arena confinement.
- Arenas (`BossArena.ts`) on terrain plateaus, a cinematic intro with letterbox and title,
  mist walls during the fight, reset on death, rewards and progress on victory.
- Three bosses: Gloomhorn (colossal beast, shadow-demon model), Vermilion (dragon-kin,
  animated red-dragon model, knocked down by parried fire), the Pale Sovereign (finale).
- Shared hazards: jumpable shockwaves, lingering zones, lane telegraphs; `hazard` hits are
  unparryable; sun/fire/moon orb kinds with reflect damage.
- Boss HUD; gate keys for Chapters II and V; the hub dragon display leaves after Vermilion.
- `npm run bosstest` (15 checks).

## Found and fixed

- No dry, flat ground exists in the Violet Fen for an arena; arenas now level a plateau in
  the analytic terrain (`WorldTerrain.addPlateau`).
- The player could stay frozen if an intro started and ended between rendered frames; the
  unfreeze moved from camera code to a director callback (`onBossBegin`).
- Area effects (lance, flash, shockwaves, zones) could be parried; they are `hazard` now.
