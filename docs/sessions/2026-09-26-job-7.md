# Job 7 — The 40-Biome Atlas & Chapter Structure

## Delivered

- 40 biomes as data variants (`src/world/biomes/Atlas.ts`) over the five archetypes;
  chapters VI–VIII remix them under three new sky presets.
- A landmark generator with ten seeded kinds tinted per biome (five new: portal, ruined
  keep, colossal bones, stone arch/aqueduct, great tree).
- Chapter gating: mist veils at the passes that open after three camps of the chapter
  are cleared, invisible walls along ridge crests, a mountain rampart (with wall) around
  the hub leaving only the northern valley, and a wall at the world's edge.
- The world map (M) with fog of war, overlays and a textual progress panel.
- `Progress` as the shared record of discoveries, cleared encounters, gates and bosses.
- `npm run atlastest` (17 checks).

## Fixed along the way

- The castle-stone material is vertex-coloured; landmark meshes without colours rendered
  black (the Wayward Watchtower since Job 6). They are now tinted.
- The great tree's canopy was one blob; it is now a wide crown of clumps and boughs.
- Four variants were too close to their archetype; each gained palette/motes/fauna changes.
