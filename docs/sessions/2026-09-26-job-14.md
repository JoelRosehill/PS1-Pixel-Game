# Session — Job 14: Bestiary (2026-09-26)

**Goal:** replace the primitive enemies with the user's animated PS1 creatures, make
normal foes Elden-Ring average and bosses hard, and turn the statue/frozen-dragon bosses
into real fights.

## What changed
- `src/enemies/Bestiary.ts` — 12 foe specs (model, weapon, tint, stats, perception,
  attack list with shapes: arc, circle, lunge, projectile, volley, breath, blink).
- `src/enemies/CreatureEnemy.ts` — one state machine for all of them (idle → alert →
  approach → circle → wind-up → strike → recover → return; staggered), poise, guard,
  armour, exposed/backstab multipliers, fliers, death clips, level scaling.
- `EnemyDirector` spawns Bestiary foes by id (`knight`/`wizard` map to Shadow Knight and
  Sunkeeper so old encounter data still works); encounters carry a level.
- `src/enemies/bosses/CreatureBoss.ts` + `Morrow`, `Gloomhorn`, `Solenne`, `Glutton`,
  `Vermilion`, `Caddoc`, `HiveQueen`, `Sovereign`; `Roster.ts` lists them.
- `World` now places all eight arenas (one per chapter).
- Deleted `ShadowKnight.ts`, `SunkeeperWizard.ts`.

## Fixes found by the tests
- Foes circled just outside their bite range (dead zone + a 2.5 m floor in
  `readyRange`) — ready foes now close all the way in.
- The bee has no `fly` clip; airborne bees use their hovering walk/run cycles.
- Solenne's sun halo covered her head; it now hangs behind her.
