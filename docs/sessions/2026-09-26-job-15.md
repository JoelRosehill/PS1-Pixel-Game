# Session — Job 15: The Long Road (2026-09-26)

**Goal (user's remake request):** replace the "SO RANDOM" radial map with one specific,
long journey through 40 biomes; use the supplied structures in the biomes they belong to,
grounded (no floating church or parkour course, nothing piled up at spawn); Elden Ring
majestic landscapes, huge structures, a humongous walking distance; keep the forest,
the stars and the sky.

## What changed
- `src/world/route/Road.ts` — the spiral road, arc-length sampling, nearest-point grid.
- `src/world/biomes/Journey.ts` — the 40 legs (biome remix, length, valley width, level,
  foes, structures, sea side, arena). `Chapters.ts` derives from it.
- `src/world/engine/WorldAtlas.ts` — rewritten: sites along the road, weights by arc
  length, valley width with gorges at chapter gates, road height profile.
- `src/world/engine/WorldTerrain.ts` — rewritten: valley relief on the road profile,
  levelled road bed, colossal mountains, sea cliffs, plaza, gridded plateaus.
- `src/world/route/Structures.ts` — placement, plateaus, loading, kit filtering, planted
  weapons, glow, double-sided environments, geometry-baked collision, scaled procedural
  landmarks. `ModelBounds.ts` (+ `tools/model-bounds.mjs`) gives footprints before load.
- `src/world/route/Dawnspire.ts`, `Atmosphere.moonAnchor`.
- `ChapterGates` (gateways over gorges), `Camps` (along the road, Bestiary foes, levels),
  `StoryProps` (shrine per biome), `World` (no hub; boundary; test site; training yard),
  `PagePickups` (pages from the level), `WorldMap` (road, spire, structures).
- Deleted: `ProvingGrounds`, `Terrain`, `Castle`, `HeroAssets`, `ParkourCourse`,
  `SunkeeperRuin` and hub-only prop helpers. `SparringConstruct` stays for tests.

## Findings along the way
- Nearest-point parametrisation jumps inside sharp corners; the approach to the plaza is
  now a curve of ≥ 260 m radius (the border-continuity test found it).
- Offset-polyline walls leave gaps on curves; replaced by an analytic boundary in the
  collision world.
- The creepy-forest environment faces inward; it is drawn double-sided.
- A raised plateau's blend must be included in its clearance from the road.
