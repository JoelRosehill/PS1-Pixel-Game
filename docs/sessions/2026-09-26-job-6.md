# Job 6 — World Engine & Biome Framework

## Delivered

- **World** (`src/world/World.ts`) replaces the single diorama as the game's `Level`. The
  Threshold remains, unchanged inside 380 m, as the hub of a 9 km world: eight chapter
  sectors × five biome sites, ridges with passes, world-edge mountains.
- **Engine** (`src/world/engine/`): `WorldAtlas` (layout, soft-Voronoi weights),
  `WorldTerrain` (analytic, blended ground and colour), `TerrainStreamer` (quadtree tiles
  with LOD following the Smart-Pixel bands), `PropStreamer` + `PropLibrary` (three rings,
  deterministic scattering, collider groups), `Camps`, `Ambience`.
- **Biomes as data** (`src/world/biomes/`): the five archetypes fully built, each with a
  landmark builder; three new sky presets; biome-driven sky/fog crossfades.
- `ColliderWorld` collider groups (stream in/out); `Atmosphere.setWeights`; range-clipped
  prop materials; `npm run worldtest` (19 checks).

## Bugs and design corrections found on the way

- A 38 m seam where each pass ramp ended: the ramp was computed only inside the ridge's
  130 m falloff while its corridor extends 200 m. Found by the zoomed continuity test.
- Terrace risers were 3.2 m near-vertical steps — taller than a held jump. Now 2 m steps
  with softer edges.
- Camp spawns could land in marsh pools; spawns now search for dry ground.
- The Spire Citadel's crag blocked the hub's only exit once the world opened up. It moved
  to twice the distance at twice the size, so it still rises against the moon from spawn.
- Landmark point lights would have multiplied every material's light loop; the world now
  lends a fixed pool of three lights to the nearest landmark anchors.
- First pass drew ~250 terrain tiles and ~590k prop triangles; retuned to 32×32 tiles with
  36 m leaves and simplified far-ring props.

## Verification
