# 2026-09-26 — Remake Jobs 11 and 12

The user played Jobs 1–10 and asked for a remake (full feedback in ROADMAP Part II).

## Job 11 — Creature Forge
- Installed Blender as a Python module (`pip install bpy==4.2.0`, Python 3.11) in a
  scratch virtualenv; no GUI Blender needed.
- Discovered: the red dragon's ten "animations" are single-frame poses, and its hands,
  feet and wing hands are IK targets parented to the root — which is why it looked frozen.
  They are re-parented into FK chains before animating.
- The horse's armature carried a stretching object scale (3.8 × 2.6 × 0.78); removing it
  gives a proper horse.
- The shadow demon and the wanderer, bee, chicken and bingus had no rig: skeletons were
  placed from height-slice clusters and orthographic renders, skinned by distance with
  side/limb rules, and (demon) IK-straightened out of the sculpted lunge.
- Blender's NLA export writes a channel per bone per clip (the dragon GLB was 4.7 MB,
  mostly JSON); `strip.mjs` removes still channels (dragon → 1.1 MB).
- Verification: Cycles contact sheets per clip (reviewed), `npm run creaturetest` 63/63.
